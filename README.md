Campfire is an AI-native ERP built for high-growth startups and mid-market finance teams outgrowing QuickBooks or Xero, or replacing NetSuite and Sage Intacct. It automates core accounting work — general ledger, bank reconciliation, accounts payable and receivable, revenue recognition, close management, and financial reporting — with an AI assistant (Ember). Campfire ships developer-first REST APIs (188 paths / 362 operations across Cash Management, Core Accounting, Accounts Payable/Receivable, Revenue Recognition, Financial Statements, and Integrations), an official hosted Model Context Protocol (MCP) server, and outbound webhooks. Founded 2023; backed by Accel, Ribbit Capital, Foundation Capital, Capital49, and Y Combinator.

**Areas:** Company · Accounting · ERP · Finance · Revenue Recognition · Accounts Payable · Accounts Receivable · Artificial Intelligence

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Campfire: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
